First, the big idea

Design is not decoration.

Graphic design is the job of arranging pictures, words, colour and space so that someone understands something and feels something, usually in a few seconds and usually without noticing you did it.

A poster, an app screen, a cereal box, a logo and an Instagram carousel are all the same game. You have a message, a person and a tiny window of attention. The designer's job is to make the important thing obvious, the next thing easy and the whole thing feel right for the brand.

Explain like I'm 10

Imagine your room is a mess and your mum asks for your football boots. Design is tidying the room so the boots are the first thing she sees, and making it look nice enough that she smiles.

The good news is that design is not a talent you're born with. It is a set of learnable rules (psychology, colour, type, layout) plus taste, which you can train like a muscle. This guide teaches both, in that order.

Why it matters: the research

People judge with their eyes, fast.

50msis enough for people to form a stable judgement of how visually appealing a web page is. That's faster than a blink. (Lindgaard et al., 2006)
46%of people's comments about a website's credibility were about how it looked: layout, type, colour. (Stanford Web Credibility Project, Fogg et al., 2002)
Red ≠ BlueAcross four studies, red pushed perceived "excitement" up and blue pushed "competence" up. (Labrecque & Milne, 2012)
$98kUS median pay for web & digital interface designers (graphic designers: $61,300). Source: US Bureau of Labor Statistics, May 2024.

Every number in this guide links back to a real study, and where a famous stat is shakier than people claim, I'll tell you. Being sceptical is part of having taste.
