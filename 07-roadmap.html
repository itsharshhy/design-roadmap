Zero to portfolio in twelve months.

An hour or two a day, six phases, about 40 milestones. Tick them as you go: your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back any time.

The daily rhythm

Every day, about 70 minutes

20min

Look

Add to your swipe file. Dissect one piece: what, how, why.

40min

Make

Copy, remix or create. Always finish something, however small.

10min

Share

Post it, ask one question, give feedback to someone else.

Open your eyes

Goal: start seeing design everywhere and get comfortable in Figma. Read chapters 01–02.

Months 2–3

The foundations

Goal: understand colour, type and layout deeply. Read chapters 03–05. Copy a lot.

You'll haveReal control over colour, type and layout, plus your first before/after redesign.

Months 4–5

Tools & craft

Goal: get fast and clean in your tools. Tools don't make designers, but slow tools hold them back.

You'll haveSpeed, clean vectors, a visible streak of public work, and a growing audience.

Months 6–8

Real projects

Goal: three portfolio-grade projects, each with research, process and final work. Read chapter 06.

You'll haveThree case studies that prove you can think, not just decorate.
05
Months 9–10

Pick your lane

Goal: go deeper in one specialisation. Generalists get you started; a speciality gets you hired.

You'll haveA clear "I'm a ___ designer" sentence and work that backs it up.

Months 11–12

Go pro

Goal: package the work, show it to the world and land your first paid work.

You'll haveA portfolio, a public presence, and your first income from design.

Glossary

Speak designer

Hierarchy
The order in which the eye notices things. Biggest and boldest first.
Whitespace
Empty space around and between things. Not necessarily white.
Grid
Invisible columns and rows that things align to.
Kerning
Space between two specific letters.
Tracking
Even spacing across a whole word or line.
Leading
Space between lines of text (line-height).
Hue / Saturation / Value
Which colour, how intense, how light or dark.
Contrast ratio
How different text and background are in lightness. 4.5:1 minimum for body text.
Vector
Graphics made of maths (paths), so they scale to any size. Logos are always vectors.
Raster
Graphics made of pixels (photos). They go blurry when enlarged.
Moodboard
A collage that captures the feeling of a project before designing.
Brief
A one-page agreement on the problem, goal, audience and deliverables.
Brand guidelines
The rulebook for how a brand looks: logo use, colours, type, tone.
Bleed
Extra image beyond the trim edge in print, so nothing white peeks out after cutting.
Mockup
Your design placed on a realistic object (phone, bag, billboard).
Wireframe
A rough black-and-white layout sketch, all structure and no style.
Design system
Reusable components and rules so a big product stays consistent.
Case study
A portfolio story: problem → process → solution → result.
